# Primordial perturbations and the Mukhanov-Sasaki benchmark

Scalar perturbations in canonical single-field inflation can be packaged into the Mukhanov-Sasaki variable $v_k$, satisfying

$$
v_k''+\left(k^2-\frac{z''}{z}\right)v_k=0,
\qquad
z=a\frac{\dot\phi}{H},
$$

where primes denote conformal-time derivatives.

Tensor modes obey an analogous equation with $z$ replaced by the scale factor $a$ for each polarization.

For exact de Sitter space,

$$
\frac{z''}{z}\rightarrow\frac{2}{\eta^2}
$$

in the standard massless benchmark, giving

$$
v_k''+\left(k^2-\frac{2}{\eta^2}\right)v_k=0.
$$

The Bunch-Davies solution is

$$
v_k(\eta)=\frac{e^{-ik\eta}}{\sqrt{2k}}
\left(1-\frac{i}{k\eta}\right).
$$

Phase 5 includes a numerical integration of this equation and a test that the numerical late-time amplitude agrees with the analytic solution. This is deliberately a **benchmark problem**. A later phase will couple the mode equations to arbitrary numerically evolved inflationary backgrounds.

At first slow-roll order,

$$
\mathcal P_{\mathcal R}\simeq
\frac{H_*^2}{8\pi^2M_{\rm Pl}^2\epsilon_*},
\qquad
\mathcal P_T\simeq\frac{2H_*^2}{\pi^2M_{\rm Pl}^2},
$$

and hence

$$
r\simeq16\epsilon_*.
$$
