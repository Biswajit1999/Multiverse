# Why did the hot Big Bang happen?

## Status: open problem

The shortest scientifically defensible answer is:

> **We do not currently know the unique physical cause of the hot Big Bang state.**

The standard hot Big Bang model describes the expansion and thermal evolution of an already hot, dense universe. It is extraordinarily successful after that state is established, but its Friedmann equations do not contain a term labelled "cause of the universe."

The useful question is therefore to ask which earlier dynamics could *generate* that hot state.

## Mechanism 1 — Reheating after inflation

Inflation can dilute pre-existing matter and leave the universe dominated by the energy of a scalar field. The hot phase is then produced when that energy is transferred into particles.

$$
\dot\rho_\phi+3H(1+w_\phi)\rho_\phi=-\Gamma_\phi\rho_\phi
$$

$$
\dot\rho_R+4H\rho_R=\Gamma_\phi\rho_\phi
$$

When $\rho_R>\rho_\phi$, the radiation-dominated hot Big Bang description becomes appropriate.

This answers a **transition** question: how a vacuum-dominated state can become hot. It does not explain why the inflating state or its initial conditions existed.

## Mechanism 2 — Bubble nucleation

A metastable false vacuum can decay by quantum tunneling. In an inflating background, local bubble interiors can thermalize while the surrounding false vacuum continues expanding. In suitable models this produces many disconnected reheating regions.

The semiclassical nucleation rate is

$$
\Gamma/V\sim A e^{-B/\hbar}.
$$

This can generate a local "big bang-like" beginning inside a bubble without making the surrounding spacetime begin at the same event.

## Mechanism 3 — A bounce

Modified high-density dynamics may turn a contracting branch into expansion.

A standard effective example is

$$
H^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho\left(1-\frac{\rho}{\rho_c}\right).
$$

At $\rho=\rho_c$ the Hubble parameter vanishes and the universe can pass through a nonzero minimum scale factor. The hot Big Bang then follows a bounce rather than an initial singularity.

## Mechanism 4 — Quantum boundary proposals

Quantum cosmology tries to replace a classical initial singularity with a wave-function boundary condition. Examples include Hartle-Hawking no-boundary and Vilenkin tunneling prescriptions.

These frameworks address the boundary-condition problem but currently lack decisive observational selection.

## Mechanism 5 — Cyclic / ekpyrotic transition

In cyclic models the hot phase follows a previous contracting epoch. The model must explain smoothing, entropy, perturbation transfer, and the transition between contraction and expansion.

## What would distinguish them?

A model becomes scientifically useful only when it predicts observables: $n_s$, $r$, non-Gaussianity, gravitational waves, curvature, topology, or specific power-spectrum features. The project therefore treats "why did the Big Bang happen?" as a **model-discrimination problem**, not as a rhetorical question.
