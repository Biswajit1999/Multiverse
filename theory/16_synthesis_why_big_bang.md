# Synthesis: why did the Big Bang happen?

The project now reaches a sharper conclusion than the phrase "we do not know what came before the Big Bang."

## What is established

Observations strongly support an expanding universe that passed through an early hot, dense, radiation-dominated state. Standard cosmology successfully describes nucleosynthesis, recombination, structure growth and the late-time universe from that state onward.

## What remains unexplained

The standard hot-Big-Bang equations do not uniquely determine why there was an expanding solution rather than another initial state, whether a classical spacetime existed earlier, whether the past boundary is singular, bouncing or quantum, whether inflation occurred and which microscopic field generated it, whether inflation was eternal in some regions, or whether other causally disconnected reheating regions exist.

## Concrete candidate mechanisms

**Inflation + reheating** can dynamically produce a hot radiation bath from a vacuum-dominated phase. **False-vacuum decay** can nucleate bubble interiors in an inflating background. **Bounce/cyclic models** can replace a singular boundary with a contracting predecessor. **Quantum-cosmology proposals** replace an ordinary classical initial condition by a wave function or boundary prescription.

None has yet been established as the unique physical origin of our universe.

## The research answer

The physically meaningful question is therefore not simply "what is outside the wall?" The wall is a causal horizon, not a material boundary. The research problem is:

> For each proposed extension of the hot Big Bang, derive its dynamics, calculate its observable predictions, determine its mathematical and statistical failure conditions, and compare it with the data and with competing models.

That is the problem this repository is designed to solve incrementally.
