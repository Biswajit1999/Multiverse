# Multiverse frameworks to investigate

This project will compare, without conflating them:

1. Eternal inflation and pocket universes
2. False-vacuum decay and bubble nucleation
3. String-theory landscape proposals
4. Tegmark's multiverse taxonomy
5. Quantum cosmology / Wheeler-DeWitt approaches
6. Hartle-Hawking no-boundary and tunneling proposals
7. Ekpyrotic and cyclic cosmologies
8. Loop-quantum-cosmology-inspired bounces
9. Conformal cyclic cosmology
10. Many-worlds quantum mechanics versus cosmological multiverse ideas

Each framework will be documented with assumptions, equations, observables, critiques, and a scientific-status tag.
