# Inflation and eternal inflation

## Inflation

Inflation is accelerated expansion, $\ddot a>0$, often modeled with a scalar field. A canonical homogeneous field satisfies

$$
\ddot\phi+3H\dot\phi+V_{,\phi}=0
$$

Its energy density and pressure are

$$
\rho_\phi=\tfrac12\dot\phi^2+V(\phi),\qquad
p_\phi=\tfrac12\dot\phi^2-V(\phi)
$$

Potential domination can therefore drive $p\approx-\rho$, yielding accelerated expansion.

## Eternal inflation

In some inflationary models, quantum fluctuations of the inflaton can dominate over the classical field displacement in sufficiently inflating regions. Inflation can then continue in some domains while ending in others. The reheated regions may become causally disconnected and are often called pocket universes.

**Important:** this is a model-dependent theoretical mechanism, not observational evidence that other universes exist.
