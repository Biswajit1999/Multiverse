# The hot Big Bang, horizons, and the word "before"

## Scientific status: Established + model-dependent extension

The hot Big Bang model describes an early universe that was hotter, denser, and more nearly homogeneous than it is today, followed by expansion and cooling. It should not automatically be identified with a mathematically proven absolute beginning of spacetime.

The **observable universe** is the region from which signals have had time to reach us. A horizon is not a material wall. The phrase "outside the universe" can therefore mean very different things: beyond our particle horizon, beyond a chosen spatial patch, an extra-dimensional embedding, or a causally disconnected spacetime region.

A central goal of this repository is to keep these meanings separate.
