# FLRW geometry and Friedmann dynamics

For a homogeneous and isotropic spacetime,

\[
ds^2=-c^2dt^2+a^2(t)\left[\frac{dr^2}{1-kr^2}+r^2d\Omega^2\right].
\]

The first Friedmann equation is

\[
H^2=\left(\frac{\dot a}{a}\right)^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho-\frac{kc^2}{a^2}+\frac{\Lambda c^2}{3}.
\]

Energy conservation gives

\[
\dot\rho+3H\left(\rho+\frac{p}{c^2}\right)=0.
\]

For an equation of state \(p=w\rho c^2\), one obtains \(\rho\propto a^{-3(1+w)}\).
