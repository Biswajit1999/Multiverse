# What we know / infer / do not know

## What we know observationally
- The universe is expanding.
- The cosmic microwave background exists and is highly isotropic with measured anisotropies.
- Primordial light-element abundances broadly support a hot early phase.
- Large-scale structure grew from small early density perturbations.

## What we infer within standard cosmology
- A hot, dense early phase.
- A cosmological history well described at late times by an FLRW background plus perturbations.

## What remains model-dependent or unknown
- The fundamental origin of inflation, if inflation occurred.
- Whether inflation was eternal.
- Whether causally disconnected universes exist.
- Whether the hot Big Bang was preceded by another phase.
- Whether spacetime has an absolute beginning.
- The correct quantum theory of gravity.

## Claims this project must not make
- That the multiverse has been observed.
- That "before the Big Bang" has a known unique answer.
- That an illustrative bubble-universe image is a physical photograph.
- That any one speculative model is established fact.
