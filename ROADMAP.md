## What “complete” means

Phase 10 does not mean that cosmic origins or the multiverse are solved. It means the repository now contains a coherent, reproducible research framework spanning the major model classes requested at the start of the project.

The next work is genuine research extension rather than a missing project phase: generic CDL boundary-value searches and negative-mode analysis, arbitrary-background primordial perturbations, full experiment likelihoods, bubble-collision spacetime simulations, and more complete quantum-gravity dynamics.
