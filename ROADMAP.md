## What Phase 5 means

Phase 5 is the point at which the repository begins to connect early-universe dynamics to measurable quantities rather than only illustrating background expansion. It now contains:

- numerical conformal-time and horizon integrals;
- particle/event horizon and comoving-Hubble-radius diagnostics;
- reusable quadratic and Starobinsky-type inflation potentials;
- numerical slow-roll end conditions and e-fold inversion;
- $(n_s,r)$ parameter sweeps;
- scalar/tensor slow-roll spectrum utilities;
- a numerical Mukhanov-Sasaki de-Sitter benchmark tested against its analytic solution;
- notebooks and figures reproducing these calculations.

See [RESULTS_PHASE5.md](RESULTS_PHASE5.md) for quantitative results.

Phase 10 does **not** mean that the multiverse is solved. It means the repository has become a mature, reproducible investigation of competing cosmic-origin models, with assumptions, failure conditions and observable consequences stated explicitly.
