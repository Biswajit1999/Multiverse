# Figure attribution and reuse

The figures in this directory are part of the **Multiverse Cosmology Lab** visual research presentation by **Biswajit Jana (2026)**.

## Preferred citation

When reusing a figure, please cite it as:

> Jana, B. (2026). *Multiverse Cosmology Lab*, Figure XX: [figure title]. https://github.com/Biswajit1999/Multiverse

Each figure on the live website now displays its figure number, title, and **© 2026 Biswajit Jana** attribution, and the full-size viewer includes a copyable citation.

## Scientific-status note

These images are **conceptual / explanatory research illustrations** unless explicitly identified as measured data. They must not be represented as telescope images, direct observations of other universes, or substitutes for the cited numerical/data products.

Measured CMB plots on the website use public NASA/LAMBDA plotting data and remain attributable to the relevant experiments and archive.

## Rights and practical limits

The authored captions, arrangement, research narrative, and project presentation are © 2026 Biswajit Jana. Any underlying scientific equations, public data, and published results remain attributable to their original sources.

Browser controls can discourage casual saving, but a publicly served image cannot be made technically impossible to copy, inspect, screenshot, or retrieve. The website therefore prioritizes clear attribution, citation, and provenance rather than claiming a perfect technical copy lock.
