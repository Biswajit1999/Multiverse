THE PHYSICAL PATH

From a nearly empty inflating state to a hot universe full of particles.

The critical step is reheating. Inflation alone leaves a cold, vacuum-dominated universe. A viable origin story has to transfer energy into radiation and then connect those early fluctuations to the structure we observe billions of years later.

THE WALLS OF THE ROOM

The observable universe is not known to have a material wall.

The “wall” is causal: light has had only finite time to reach us through an expanding spacetime. Regions beyond our particle horizon can exist without being separate universes. Bubble universes are a stronger, model-dependent statement.

THE ALTERNATIVES ARE NOT DELETED — THEY ARE PUT ON TRIAL

Inflation, bounce, cyclic evolution, and a quantum boundary answer different versions of “before”.

Inflation + reheatingcomplete bridge to hot phase + observable perturbations
Bounceexplicit nonsingular background; high-density completion and perturbations remain model dependent
Cyclicrequires a repeatable transition, entropy control and viable perturbations
Quantum boundarychanges the initial-condition problem; interpretation and measure remain open

AND THE MULTIVERSE?

A consequence in some models, not the answer I smuggled in at the start.

When quantum fluctuations overpower classical inflaton drift in some regions, inflation can become self-reproducing. Reheating then ends inflation locally while other regions continue expanding.

𝓡EI ≃ 3H³ / (2π|V,φ|)

If 𝓡EI > 1 locally, the stochastic regime becomes possible. That is a theoretical criterion. It is not a telescope image of another universe.

AUTHOR'S SYNTHESIS · 2026

If forced to compress the entire investigation to one word:

INFLATION

followed by reheating.

It is the strongest working bridge I find between a pre-hot-Big-Bang state and the hot, structured universe we can measure. The deeper question — what selected the inflating state, and whether its global completion contains other universes — remains open.
