FIELD NOTE 001 · EARLY-UNIVERSE COSMOLOGY · 2026

What happened before the hot Big Bang?

I approached the problem backwards: begin with the relics we can measure, evolve candidate histories, and ask which earlier state can actually produce the hot universe we observe.

ONE-WORD WORKING ANSWER Inflation.

More precisely: inflation followed by reheating is the strongest testable bridge found in this project. It is a working conclusion, not proof of an absolute beginning or of a multiverse.

THE ARGUMENT IN ONE PAGE

The useful question is not “which theory sounds beautiful?” It is “which history survives contact with physics?”

The standard hot-Big-Bang model begins with an already hot, dense, expanding state. That framework is extraordinarily successful at describing what follows, but it does not by itself tell us which physical process produced that state.

Inflation changes the problem. A scalar field can drive accelerated expansion; quantum fluctuations generated during that phase can seed the primordial spectrum; reheating can transfer energy from the inflating field into particles and radiation. That gives one continuous calculable chain from a pre-hot state to observables.

My current synthesis is deliberately narrower than “inflation is proven”: among the mechanisms implemented here, inflation + reheating gives the strongest end-to-end route from pre-hot dynamics to measurements.

FROM QUANTUM SEEDS TO GALAXIES

A theory of “before” earns its place by leaving fingerprints “after”.

That is the central methodological rule of this project. The origin problem is not solved by adding a more exotic past. It is solved only when a proposed past leads to predictions that can be compared with the universe we actually inhabit.

SO WHERE DOES THE MULTIVERSE ENTER?

As a possible consequence of some inflationary dynamics — not as an assumption.

A useful local self-reproduction diagnostic compares quantum fluctuations of the inflaton with its classical drift:

𝓡EI ≃ 3H³ / (2π|V,φ|)

If 𝓡EI > 1 in part of field space, stochastic self-reproduction can occur in suitable models. Local regions may reheat while other regions continue inflating.

What this does not mean We do not have a telescope image, coordinate, or direct detection of another pocket universe.
AUTHOR'S SYNTHESIS · 2026 If I compress the investigation to one word: INFLATION followed by reheating

It is the strongest working bridge I find between a pre-hot-Big-Bang state and the hot, structured universe we can measure.

The deeper question — what selected the inflating state, and whether its global completion contains other universes — remains open.
