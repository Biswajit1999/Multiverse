THE PHYSICAL STORY

Start with what survived. Reconstruct what could have come before.

The narrative is deliberately ordered from measured relics back toward progressively more model-dependent physics.

The standard hot-Big-Bang model begins with an already hot, dense, expanding state. The sharper problem is therefore: what physical process can generate that state and still leave the relics we measure today?

Inflation supplies accelerated expansion. Quantum fluctuations become curvature perturbations. Reheating transfers energy into particles and radiation. The calculation then enters the thermal history tested by the CMB.

COMPETING HISTORIES

“Before” is not one theory.

Inflationary extension, bounce/cyclic histories and quantum-boundary proposals answer different questions and carry different assumptions.

01 · WORKING LEAD

Inflation + reheating

Accelerated expansion followed by transfer of energy into radiation. It supplies a physical route into the hot Big Bang and observable perturbations.

nₛ ≃ 1 − 6εᵥ + 2ηᵥ · r ≃ 16εᵥ
02 · ALTERNATIVE

Nonsingular bounce

A contracting phase reaches a finite-density turnaround. The difficult tests are high-density completion, perturbation stability and distinctive signatures.

H² ∝ ρ(1 − ρ/ρc)
03 · ALTERNATIVE

Cyclic cosmology

Repeated contraction and expansion can replace an absolute classical beginning, but entropy and transition physics must remain controlled.

a(t): contraction → transition → expansion
04 · QUANTUM BOUNDARY

Quantum cosmology

No-boundary and tunnelling proposals replace an ordinary earlier classical phase with a quantum boundary prescription.

ĤΨ = 0

CAUSAL STRUCTURE

“Outside our universe” is not automatically a meaningful coordinate.

Ordinary FLRW cosmology can contain regions beyond our present particle horizon without those regions being separate universes. Eternal-inflation “pockets” are a different, model-dependent construction.

χp(t) = c ∫ dt′ / a(t′)

A horizon is a causal boundary, not a material shell.

WHERE THE MULTIVERSE ENTERS

A conditional consequence of some dynamics — not the starting assumption.

Some inflationary potentials can enter a stochastic self-reproduction regime. Local regions may reheat while other regions continue inflating.

ℛEI ≃ 3H³ / (2π|V,φ|)

When this local diagnostic exceeds unity in a suitable part of field space, stochastic fluctuations can dominate classical drift. Eternal inflation may then occur in that model.

Evidence boundaryNo other pocket universe has been directly observed. There is no astronomical coordinate pointing to a confirmed external universe.

COULD THERE BE AN “OUTSIDE” PERSPECTIVE?

A useful speculative question, but standard cosmology does not give the whole universe an ordinary outside.

Some higher-dimensional or brane constructions embed lower-dimensional spacetimes in a larger mathematical geometry. That can make an external embedding meaningful inside a specific theory.

It does not imply literal beings outside our universe, nor does current evidence establish an external observing vantage point.
