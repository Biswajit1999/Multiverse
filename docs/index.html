THE ARGUMENT

The Big Bang is not the first line of the calculation.

The standard hot-Big-Bang model begins with an already hot, dense, expanding state. The origin problem is therefore sharper: what physical process can produce that state and still leave the relics we measure today?

Inflation supplies accelerated expansion; quantum fluctuations seed curvature perturbations; reheating converts the inflating sector into particles and radiation. That is a continuous calculable chain into the thermal universe.

COMPETING HISTORIES

“Before” is not one theory.

Inflationary extension, bounce/cyclic histories and quantum-boundary proposals answer different questions and carry different assumptions.

01 · WORKING LEAD

Inflation + reheating

Accelerated expansion followed by transfer of energy into a radiation bath. It provides the project’s strongest calculable route into the hot Big Bang and produces perturbation observables.

nₛ ≃ 1 − 6εᵥ + 2ηᵥ   ·   r ≃ 16εᵥ
02 · ALTERNATIVE

Nonsingular bounce

A contracting phase reaches a finite-density turnaround. The hard problems are the high-density completion, perturbation stability and distinctive observational signatures.

H² ∝ ρ(1 − ρ/ρc)
03 · ALTERNATIVE

Cyclic cosmology

Repeated contraction and expansion can replace an absolute classical beginning, but entropy, transition physics and perturbations must all remain controlled.

a(t): contraction → transition → expansion
04 · QUANTUM BOUNDARY

Quantum cosmology

No-boundary and tunnelling proposals replace an ordinary earlier classical phase with a quantum boundary prescription. Their empirical interpretation remains open.

ĤΨ = 0

FALSE VACUUM → BUBBLE INTERIOR

If bubbles matter, the tunnelling calculation has to come first.

The project treats bubble universes as field-theory objects: specify a potential, solve the Euclidean problem, estimate the action, then ask what happens inside the nucleated region.

CAUSAL STRUCTURE

“Outside our universe” is not automatically a meaningful coordinate.

In ordinary cosmology, there can be regions beyond our present particle horizon without those regions being separate universes. In eternal-inflation models, “pocket universes” instead refer to causally separated reheating regions in a larger inflating spacetime.

χp(t) = c ∫ dt′ / a(t′)

A horizon is a causal boundary, not a material shell.

WHERE THE MULTIVERSE ENTERS

As a conditional consequence of some dynamics — not as the starting assumption.

Some inflationary potentials can enter a stochastic self-reproduction regime. Local regions may reheat while other regions continue inflating. That is one mathematical route to many causally disconnected regions.

ℛEI ≃ 3H³ / (2π|V,φ|)

When this local diagnostic exceeds unity in a suitable part of field space, stochastic fluctuations can dominate classical drift. Eternal inflation may then occur in that model.

Evidence boundary No other pocket universe has been directly observed. There is no astronomical coordinate pointing to a confirmed external universe.

COULD THERE BE AN “OUTSIDE” PERSPECTIVE?

A useful speculative question, but standard cosmology does not give the whole universe an ordinary outside.

Some higher-dimensional or brane constructions embed lower-dimensional spacetimes in a larger mathematical geometry. That can make an external embedding meaningful inside a specific theory.

It does not imply that literal beings exist outside our universe, nor does present evidence establish an external observing vantage point. The image is deliberately marked as a conceptual thought experiment.

SPECULATIVE / MODEL-DEPENDENT

WHAT WOULD MOVE THE CONCLUSION?

The project is designed to be wrong in specific ways.

The working synthesis should change if another model generates a stable hot universe with stronger observational leverage, if a distinctive competing signal is detected, or if future precision data falsify the inflationary predictions used here.
